Fonts are a great way to improve the look of your app. Flutter allows you to use custom fonts in your app.

## Projects

### 1. Your Name in Two Different Custom Fonts

Create a new Flutter project and add two custom fonts to it. Display your name in each of the two fonts.

> Hint: You can download free fonts from [Google Fonts](https://fonts.google.com)

### 2. Font in Themes

Create a new Flutter project and add two custom fonts to it. Change the app's theme so that headlines use one of the two fonts and body text uses the other (hint: `TextTheme` styles such as `headlineMedium` and `bodyMedium`). Then create a simple layout with a headline and an article.
