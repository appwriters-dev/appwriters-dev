# About Master Flutter

Namaste, welcome to your master guide to learn Dart and Flutter and become a super Flutter developer. We have used the resources scattered around the internet and brought them together for you in an organized action plan. This project-based action plan to learn Dart and Flutter is not only for beginners but also for existing developers to enhance their skills.

Our aim with this guide is to keep you on track to learn Dart and Flutter without getting overwhelmed by the resources on the internet. Each lesson will provide links to the relevant resources and give projects you need to complete in order to gain the skills of the lesson.
