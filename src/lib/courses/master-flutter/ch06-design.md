Because Flutter is cross-platform, it officially supports Material Design and Cupertino (iOS-style) design. There is community support for Microsoft's Fluent design and other design philosophies. Material Design is the design language of Android, and Cupertino follows Apple's iOS guidelines. However, you can use any design you want, on any platform. In this chapter we will focus on Material Design.

[Material Design](https://m3.material.io/) is a design language developed by Google. Flutter uses its latest version, Material 3, by default. It is a comprehensive guide for visual, motion, and interaction design across platforms and devices. It is a system of guidelines, components, and tools that support the best practices of user interface design.
